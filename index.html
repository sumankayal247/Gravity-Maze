KEYBOARD CONTROLS
HOW TO PLAY
You do not move the ball directly. You move the maze. Gravity pulls the ball down, so turn the maze to make the ball roll where you want.
1
Find the ballThe white ball is your player. It moves by itself because of gravity.
2
Turn the mazeHold A or ← to turn left. Hold D or → to turn right.
3
Guide the ballTurn a little, watch the ball roll, then turn back.
4
Reach the purple holeThe purple black hole is the finish. Get the ball inside it to complete the maze.
5
Play the next mazeA new maze appears automatically. Mazes get harder as you go.
Easy trick: Small movements are easier to control. Press R to restart.
PHONE CONTROLS
HOW TO PLAY
You do not move the ball with your fingers. Tilt your phone and gravity will move the ball for you.
1
Hold your phone flatHold it level. This is your starting position.
2
Turn on phone tiltTap ENABLE PHONE TILT and choose Allow if asked.
3
Set your starting positionKeep the phone level and tap RECALIBRATE PHONE.
4
Tilt the phoneTilt left/right to move sideways. Tilt the top toward or away from you to move forward/back.
5
Find the purple holeGuide the ball into it. The next maze starts by itself.
Remember: You tilt the phone, not the maze. Gentle tilts give better control.
