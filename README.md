A physics-based 2D maze game where you **rotate the maze instead of controlling the ball directly**. The ball moves under gravity, and your job is to guide it into the black-hole exit.

> **Work in progress:** gameplay, physics, visuals, procedural generation, and level progression are still being actively developed.

## Current Concept

- Rotate the maze with **A / D** or **← / →**.
- The ball follows gravity and reacts to the rotating maze.
- Procedural mazes are designed to remain solvable.
- Maze complexity increases gradually as the tile size decreases and the maze grows.
- Completing a maze transitions into the next floor.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `A` / `←` | Rotate maze left |
| `D` / `→` | Rotate maze right |
| `R` | Restart current maze |

## Status

This repository is an active prototype. Expect changes, unfinished systems, balance adjustments, and visual/physics experimentation.
